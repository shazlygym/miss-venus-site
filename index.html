01 / COMPANY OVERVIEW

Experience you can build a collection on.

Miss Venus Factory is an Egyptian women's garment manufacturer established in 1991. With two manufacturing facilities and a workforce of approximately 500 employees, we serve apparel production requirements for local and regional markets, with an export focus across Arab countries.

Our capabilities span multiple women's apparel categories, supported by dedicated departments for product development, pattern making, cutting, sewing, knitting, quality control, finishing and packaging.

Our focus: flexible garment manufacturing solutions for buyers, whether through full-package production or CMT, following buyer-provided technical and quality requirements.

06 / MANUFACTURING SOLUTIONS

Flexible sourcing and production models.

Full Package Manufacturing (FPM)

We source fabrics and materials and manage manufacturing through finishing and packaging, based on the buyer's requirements and agreed scope.

CMT – Cut, Make & Trim

The client supplies fabrics and materials; we handle garment manufacturing and the agreed finishing processes.

Additional services: printing, embroidery and garment washing through a combination of in-house capabilities and specialized external suppliers, depending on the order.

07 / QUALITY & PRODUCTION CONTROL

Quality requirements built around each order.

A dedicated Quality Control department works according to the technical and quality requirements specified by our buyers.

  • Production follows buyer-provided specifications and tech packs
  • Size charts and measurements provided by the buyer
  • Fabric and colour standards followed per order
  • AQL inspection requirements accommodated
  • Packing and labelling instructions followed

08 / EXPORT & INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS

Connecting Egyptian manufacturing with regional markets.

We export to Arab countries and can arrange export operations directly or through export and logistics partners, according to the agreement for each order.
